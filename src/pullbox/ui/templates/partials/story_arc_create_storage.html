Story arc files

Original files stay in their series folders. A separate arc folder holds extra copies or links. Choose its storage policy now; moving an established arc to another destination isn't available yet.

Choose an existing directory inside the selected library root. Pullbox creates the named arc folder inside it.

Example with two digits: 01 - Batman 001.cbz. Choose enough digits for the arc's reading order.

Include {ReadingOrder:02d} at the start for a reading-order prefix, or end with {OriginalFilename} to keep the current filename and extension.
