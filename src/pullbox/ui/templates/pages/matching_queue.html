Good matching habits

Use the clues in the filename, then confirm the issue details.

1

Search by the parsed title first

2

Use the issue number to verify

Treat the issue number, year, and publisher as confirmation, not just loose hints.

3

Skip uncertain matches

A file can stay in the queue longer. It is better to wait than to link it to the wrong issue.
